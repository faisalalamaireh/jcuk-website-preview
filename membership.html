Become a member of JCUK

Four membership categories, from £5 a year. The Membership Committee reviews applications within 30 days, then your digital membership card arrives by email.

Choose the category that matches your situation. Only Full Members vote at general meetings and stand for the Board of Directors.

How to join

  1. Choose your categoryUse the table above. If you are unsure, email info@jcuk.org.uk.
  2. Apply onlineFill in the online form and state which category you are applying for.
  3. ApprovalThe Membership Committee reviews applications within 30 days.
  4. Receive your cardYour digital card and member number arrive by email.

Start your application

Your rights as a Full Member

  • Attend, speak and vote at general meetings, with one vote on each resolution.
  • Stand for election to the Board of Directors after 3 months of Full Membership.
  • Nominate and second candidates in Board elections.

Membership is governed by Article III of the Articles of Association, which also covers the code of conduct, political neutrality and appeals.

JCUK WhatsApp group

Members can join the official JCUK WhatsApp group to stay connected, share reliable information and hear about programmes and events. The group is a respectful space for constructive discussion about matters relevant to the community. Please read the welcome note and group guidelines before joining.
