Jordan–UK Business Forum opens in London

Under the patronage of His Majesty King Abdullah II, officials, investors and businesses from both countries met in London.

Jordan & UK6 August 20261 min read

Under the patronage of His Majesty King Abdullah II, the Jordan–United Kingdom Business Forum opened in London, bringing together government officials, investors and business representatives from Jordan and the United Kingdom to strengthen economic cooperation, foster strategic partnerships and explore new commercial opportunities across key sectors.

The Embassy of the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan in London welcomed government officials, business leaders, investors and industry representatives from both countries for a day dedicated to strengthening bilateral trade and investment.
