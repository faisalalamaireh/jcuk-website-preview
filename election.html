Elect the first Board of Directors

Members will choose the Board that takes over from the Founding Council. Voting takes place in the week beginning Monday 23 November 2026 (date to be confirmed).

Who runs the election

An independent Election Committee administers the election to ensure neutrality, transparency and procedural integrity. A Returning Officer supervises the whole process and makes sure it is conducted fairly and as JCUK's governing documents require. Once the Board is elected, the Founding Council's role ends.

How to stand

  1. Check the criteria belowYou must meet every one of them on the nomination deadline.
  2. Find a nominator and a seconderBoth must be Full Members of JCUK. They sign Section B of the nomination form.
  3. Complete the nomination formYour details, a candidate statement of up to 300 words, and the signed candidate declaration.
  4. Attach your CV and position paperA brief CV and a position paper of up to 300 words on your vision and the projects you would pursue.
  5. Sign the conflict of interest declarationElected directors update it every year.

Nomination criteria

A candidate must satisfy all of the following conditions:

  1. Be a Full Member of JCUK for at least three months before the nomination deadline.
  2. Be aged 21 or over.
  3. Be ordinarily resident in the United Kingdom for at least twelve months immediately before the nomination.
  4. Not be disqualified from acting as a director under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986 or the Companies Act 2006.
  5. Not be an undischarged bankrupt.
  6. Not have been convicted of any offence involving violence, hate, fraud, breach of trust, human rights violations, or any other offence incompatible with the duties of a director under UK law.
  7. Have sufficient English to communicate effectively with UK institutions and official bodies.
  8. Have demonstrable practical or professional experience, or active community involvement in the UK, through work, study, volunteering or community projects.
  9. Submit a brief CV and a short position paper (maximum 300 words) by the published deadline.
  10. Commit to responsible civil discourse, free from sectarianism, racism, incitement or partisan exploitation during the campaign.
  11. Sign a conflict of interest declaration before the election, and update it annually if elected.

Who can vote

Full Members vote, with one vote each. Not a Full Member yet? Apply for membership.
