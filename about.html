Who we are

JCUK serves members of the Jordanian community in the UK and strengthens their cultural, social and professional presence in British society.

JCUK has been established to serve members of the Jordanian community across a range of areas and to strengthen their cultural, social and professional presence within British society. As part of the establishment phase, a nine-member Founding Council has been formed to lead the transitional period. The Founding Council's role is to establish the organisational and administrative foundations required to convene the first General Assembly and hold elections for a Board of Directors chosen by members of the community. The role of the Founding Council will conclude upon completion of the election process.

The elections will be administered by an independent Election Committee to ensure neutrality, transparency and procedural integrity. A Returning Officer will supervise the entire process, ensuring the election is conducted fairly and as mandated by JCUK's governing documents.

Vision

A recognised community that preserves Jordanian cultural identity and builds strong, respectful bridges with UK institutions.

Values

Integrity, inclusivity, accountability, service, and respect for diversity.

Objectives

  • To provide a representative, democratic and inclusive organisation that promotes cohesion and a sense of belonging for Jordanians in the UK.
  • To preserve and promote Jordanian culture, heritage, language and traditions.
  • To support the social, educational and welfare needs of community members through educational, social and cultural programmes and initiatives.
  • To support youth, families and new arrivals to the UK.
  • To foster mutual understanding and cooperation between the Jordanian community, Jordanian institutions and UK institutions through partnerships and joint initiatives and projects.
  • To encourage civic participation and integration while respecting diversity and representing the community positively and responsibly.
  • To establish a democratic governance system through a General Assembly.

Programmes, initiatives and community services

JCUK will launch projects, initiatives and services that promote the welfare of community members and support its partners and affiliated groups. These will include:

ProgrammeWhat it offers
Cultural eventsActivities and celebrations that promote Jordanian identity and bring members of the community together in a positive social environment.
Arabic language classesEducational programmes for children and young people that preserve the Arabic language and strengthen their connection with their cultural heritage.
Youth programmesInitiatives that support Jordanian young people in education, skills development and social integration.
Women's empowerment groupsSafe spaces and development programmes that support and empower Jordanian women in the United Kingdom.
Community support servicesAdvice, guidance and help for families and newly arrived community members with housing, education, healthcare and employment.
Social gatheringsRegular gatherings that strengthen relationships among Jordanians in different cities across the United Kingdom.
Professional networksPlatforms for Jordanian professionals to connect, exchange expertise and create opportunities to work together.

These initiatives will be introduced gradually following the election of the Board of Directors. They will be open to members of the community, partners and affiliated groups.

Role of the Founding Council

The Founding Council will undertake the following responsibilities:

  • Preparing the constitution, internal regulations and organisational policies.
  • Establishing membership criteria and launching the membership registration process.
  • Designing the mechanism for electing the Board of Directors.
  • Supervising communication and engagement with members of the community.
  • Establishing the organisation's official communication channels, including its website, email and social media.
  • Ensuring compliance with legal, financial and data-protection requirements.
  • Preparing the documents required to convene the first General Assembly.
