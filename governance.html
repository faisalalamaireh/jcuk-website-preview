Articles of Association

Our constitution and bylaws set out how JCUK is run, who can be a member and how decisions are made.

Legal status

Jordanian Community in the UK is registered under number 17126706 as a Company Limited by Guarantee under the UK Companies Act 2006. Its Arabic name is الجالية الأردنية في المملكة المتحدة. JCUK operates on a non-profit basis: all income and property go towards its objects, and no surplus is distributed to members or directors.

How JCUK is governed

General Assembly

The supreme governing body, made up of all Full Members. It elects and removes Board members, approves amendments to the constitution, and receives the annual financial statements.

Board of Directors

Eleven directors, all Full Members. The Board manages JCUK's affairs, including strategic oversight, compliance with UK law, budgets and major contracts.
