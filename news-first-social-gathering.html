JCUK holds its first social gathering in London

Saturday 7 November, 3 to 6 pm, at the Hilton London Metropole. Members and new joiners welcome.

Events4 November 20261 min read

View the original poster

You are warmly invited to the first social gathering of the Jordanian Community in the UK: a chance to meet fellow Jordanians, reconnect with old friends and make new ones.

When
Saturday 7 November 2026, 3:00 to 6:00 pm
Where
Hilton London Metropole, Mezzanine Floor, 225 Edgware Road, London W2 1JU
Entry
No fee for JCUK members or for new joiners on the day

Complimentary tea, coffee and light snacks will be served. Registered members holding a membership number are warmly invited. Not yet a member? You can register on the day, subject to the membership requirements.

Please RSVP in advance with the number of attendees, including any family members, to info@jcuk.org.uk. Invitations are personal and non-transferable.
