In its founding phase, JCUK is led by a nine-member Founding Council until members elect the first Board of Directors in the week beginning Monday 23 November 2026 (date to be confirmed).

How the election works

Two homes, one community

JCUK serves Jordanians across Britain and strengthens their cultural, social and professional presence in British society.

A nine-member Founding Council is laying the organisational foundations, convening the first General Assembly and holding elections for a Board of Directors chosen by members. An independent Election Committee and a Returning Officer oversee the vote, and the Founding Council's role ends once the election is complete.

Programmes and services

Launched gradually after the Board of Directors is elected, and open to members, partners and affiliated groups.

Cultural events
Celebrations that promote Jordanian identity and bring the community together.
Arabic language classes
Lessons that keep children and young people connected to their language and heritage.
Youth programmes
Support for young Jordanians in education, skills and social integration.
Women's empowerment
Safe spaces and development programmes for Jordanian women in the UK.
Community support
Guidance on housing, education, healthcare and work for families and new arrivals.
Social gatherings
Regular meetings that connect Jordanians in cities across the UK.
Professional networks
Platforms for Jordanian professionals to connect and work together.

Stand for the first Board of Directors

Eleven seats, elected by Full Members in the week beginning 23 November 2026 (date to be confirmed).

  1. Check you are eligibleFull Member for at least 3 months, aged 21+, resident in the UK for the past 12 months.
  2. Find a nominator and seconderBoth must be Full Members of JCUK.
  3. Submit your nominationThe form, a short CV and a position paper of up to 300 words.
  4. Members voteOverseen by an independent Election Committee and a Returning Officer.
