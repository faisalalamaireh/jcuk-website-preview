JCUK opens membership registration

The Founding Council invites Jordanians across the UK to read the Articles of Association and register.

Community8 September 20261 min read

View the original poster

The Founding Council of the Jordanian Community in the UK announced that membership registration is open to Jordanians across the United Kingdom.

Chair of the Founding Council Dr Ghassan Al-Utaibi said that opening membership is a key step in moving from the founding phase to members taking an active part in running their organisation and choosing its Board of Directors. He invited Jordanians across the UK to read the Articles of Association and register through the official website.

Prospective members can read the Articles of Association, the membership categories, their conditions, and the rights and duties that come with them on the website. The Founding Council set the Board of Directors election for the week beginning Monday 23 November.

JCUK was founded as a non-profit company limited by guarantee, officially registered in the United Kingdom, to provide an institutional framework that serves Jordanians in the UK and strengthens their cultural, social and professional presence.

Reported by Petra (Jordan News Agency), Al-Ghad, Addustour, Arab London, Al Arab in UK, Alwakeel News and Jordan Zad.
